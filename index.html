Research

Before Measuring Human-Agent Teams: Validating Human References in Agent Benchmarks

2nd author · NeurIPS 2026 Human-AI Coevolution (HAIC) Workshop

Audits the reliability of the human task-difficulty baselines behind agent benchmarks like METR, SWE-bench Verified, and GAIA, and proposes a validity protocol for claims that agents can substitute for human work.

The Survey Item Predicts Human Disagreement Better Than Simulator Output Entropy

3rd author · NeurIPS 2026 SocialAgent Workshop

Shows that a small text encoder reading only a survey question predicts how much humans will disagree on it better than an LLM social simulator's own output uncertainty.
