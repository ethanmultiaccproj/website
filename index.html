PNS Guard: Geometric AI Safety

Advanced Architecture & Active Defense Mechanism

Overview: Modern AI safety largely relies on flat, linear steering vectors and superficial keyword filters that can easily be bypassed by sophisticated, multi-step adversarial prompts. PNS Guard introduces a robust 3D security "MRI" layer by mapping residual streams directly into Principal Nested Sphere (PNS) coordinates live.

How it Works: Utilizing lightweight transformer hooks at fractional model depths where refusal geometry naturally inverts, PNS Guard analyzes the heteroscedastic shape of activation clouds. By leveraging power diagram classifiers instead of fragile linear probes, it captures multi-dimensional geometric boundaries, allowing systems to deterministically block complex attacks before a single malicious token is generated.

Impact & Applications: Designed to secure high-stakes infrastructure—such as autonomous grid management, finance, and medical AI systems—PNS Guard prevents rogue agents from executing unintended instructions or bypassing safety constraints via distribution shifts.
